Spread your wings 🦅

1. Stand back so your shoulders, elbows and hips are in frame — hands can be off-screen.
2. Flap: sweep both arms down together from up high — a synced
downstroke gives lift. One arm alone does nothing.
3. Gravity pulls you down — keep flapping to stay airborne.
4. Tilt (one wing low) to bank and steer. Fly through the gates.
New concept vs the plane: lift from a synchronized downstroke, detected as the change in shoulder angle (elbow→shoulder→hip) sweeping high→low on both arms within ~0.3 s. Banking (elbow-line tilt) and flapping (symmetric vertical sweep) are orthogonal signals, so you can do both. Detection runs at camera rate; physics + render run at 60 fps. Pick the lite/full/heavy model and tune the 1-Euro jitter filter live.
