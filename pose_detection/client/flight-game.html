Become the plane ✈

1. Make sure your shoulders and elbows are in frame — hands can be off-screen.
2. Rest your elbows out a little — you are the wings.
3. Tilt the wings (dip one elbow) to bank and steer.
4. Line up and fly through the gates for points.
Concepts on show: pose + game in one page (DataBus-free, in-process — zero transport latency) · a continuous control axis derived from two keypoints (elbow-line tilt) · detection (camera-rate) decoupled from render (60 fps). Pick the lite/full/heavy model and tune the 1-Euro jitter filter live. A darker room caps the camera near 15 fps — steering stays smooth because render is independent.
